Research

Measurement and control of quantum systems; QEC (Quantum error correction)

Yuval is a theoretical physicist studying quantum measurement and its applications to superconducting quantum devices. His research focuses on understanding and improving the dynamics of quantum systems under continuous monitoring, with an emphasis on developing efficient and reliable measurement protocols. He also works on quantum error correction, exploring the potential of erasure qubits for fault-tolerant quantum computation.

About

Yuval Marcus is a PhD student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He earned his BSc in Physics and Mathematics and his MSc in Physics, specializing in Quantum Information Science and Technology, both at the Hebrew University. During his undergraduate studies, he conducted research in theoretical neuroscience before joining the group. Yuval is currently a research scientist intern at the AWS Center for Quantum Computing.
