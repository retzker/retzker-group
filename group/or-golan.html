Research

Or works on high-rate quantum error correction for superconducting qubits. Because high-rate codes need interactions between distant qubits, he develops a long-range gate between dual-rail transmons, now being tested with experimental collaborators at the AWS Center for Quantum Computing, alongside a ZZ gate between neighboring dual-rail transmons. He designs high-rate codes that take advantage of both gates.

About

Or Golan is a PhD student in the group. He did his MSc in theoretical condensed matter physics at Tel Aviv University, focusing on topological phases in number-conserving systems. Before starting his PhD, he was a senior quantum scientist at Qedma Quantum Computing, working on characterizing and controlling quantum hardware.
